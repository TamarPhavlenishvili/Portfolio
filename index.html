Who I am

I’m Tamar.

I’m a marketer by instinct, a strategist by mindset, and a hands-on person by nature.

I like being involved in the whole picture, from the first rough idea to the final result. I can build a strategy, challenge a creative direction, get involved in production, lead a team, manage the budget, and dig into the numbers to understand what actually worked.

Over the years, I’ve worked across different industries, teams, and challenges. I’ve led social media departments, managed client relationships, coordinated productions, built campaigns from scratch, and turned marketing budgets into measurable business results.

For me, creativity starts with a better question: what would make someone stop, care, or take action? I use that thinking to shape campaign concepts, content and digital experiences - then let the response show what deserves to grow.

I’m curious by nature, not afraid to get my hands dirty, and always interested in what could be done better. I care about the details, but never lose sight of the bigger picture.

I don’t just want to make marketing happen.
I want to make it mean something.
